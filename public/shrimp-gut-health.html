INSIDE THE SHRIMP

A healthy gut is the first line of disease defence.

Disease costs tropical shrimp farming up to 40% of production a year.[2] Many bacterial diseases, including Vibrio infections such as AHPND (early mortality syndrome), start by colonising the gut. Probiotics add beneficial bacteria. Prebiotics feed them. Together they restore a gut community that resists pathogens and turns more feed into growth.

SHRIMP MIDGUT · SCHEMATIC

PROBIOTIC

Competitive exclusion

Beneficial bacteria occupy attachment sites and nutrients on the gut wall, leaving less room for Vibrio.

PROBIOTIC

Quorum quenching

Some Bacillus make an enzyme (AHL-lactonase) that breaks down the signal molecules Vibrio use to coordinate attack. In trials this reduced Vibrio biofilm, gut colonisation and shrimp mortality.[6]

PREBIOTIC

Feeding the good bacteria

Non-digestible fibres such as oligosaccharides and β-glucans pass to the gut, where they feed beneficial bacteria. Probiotic and prebiotic together are called a synbiotic.[9]

BOTH

Immune priming and digestion

A balanced gut community supports the shrimp's immune response and digestive enzymes, so more feed becomes growth.

What pooled shrimp studies show

Meta-analysis of probiotic trials in shrimp. Every pooled effect was in the beneficial direction.

Toledo et al. 2019, Aquaculture.[5] The authors note that published studies lean towards positive results, so field responses will vary.
