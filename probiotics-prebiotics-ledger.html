Prebiotics: food for good bacteria
How they shape the community
Competitive exclusion
Beneficial bacteria take the attachment sites and nutrients that Salmonella, E. coli and Clostridium need.4
Selective feeding
Fibres such as mannan- and fructo-oligosaccharides pass undigested to the lower gut, where beneficial bacteria ferment them.
Antimicrobial compounds
Bacillus and lactic acid bacteria produce substances that inhibit pathogens directly.
Pathogen binding
Mannan-oligosaccharides (MOS) bind to the fimbriae that some bacteria use to attach, so those bacteria pass out instead of colonising.
Stronger gut lining
A healthy community supports taller villi and tighter junctions between gut cells, so fewer pathogens and toxins get through.
Short-chain fatty acids
Fermentation produces acids that lower gut pH, feed gut cells and discourage pathogens.
Hardy spores
Bacillus spores tolerate heat and stomach acid far better than most bacteria, which helps them survive feed processing and reach the intestine.
Synbiotic effect
Given together, probiotic and prebiotic reinforce each other.
